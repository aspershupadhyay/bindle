The v2 scenes are copied from `design/art/v2/png/`; redraw them there, then copy them here. The screen icons, people's pictures and the dark logo come from `design/art/v2/icons.py`.

People's pictures name what is drawn, never who someone is: anyone can pick any of them for anyone, and change it whenever they like. With no picture, a person shows their first letter.

Palette: the P2 tokens plus `#E89A72`, `#F0CF85` and `#7A5326`. The darkest pixel is ink `#2B2622`, never pure black.
