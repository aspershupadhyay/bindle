## Principles that never bend

1. Close the loop: every promise gets a first step and a when.
2. Capture in under three seconds, and show "Saved" only after it is saved.
3. Never shame: no red for your own late promises, no streaks, no scores on Home.
4. You decide: AI and automation only suggest; suggestions wait in Sort.
5. Private by design: local first, tokens in the keychain, cloud content encrypted on the device.
6. Bindle never sends email or posts to social media for you.
